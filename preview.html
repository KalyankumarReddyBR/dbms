Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to book a vehicle?

You will need an active registered account, a valid driver's license number, and a valid credit/debit card, UPI, or Net Banking credentials for deposit holds.

How do GPS simulations work on my active rentals?

When you confirm a booking, the active rental status will showcase a real-time coordinates tracking widget on your Customer Dashboard. This simulates telemetry speeds, routing updates, and coordinates mapping.

Are maintenance status logs shared transparently?

Absolutely. Admin panels trace scheduler queues. If a vehicle undergoes oil service or inspections, its status updates in real-time, removing it from catalog reservations until it is resolved.
